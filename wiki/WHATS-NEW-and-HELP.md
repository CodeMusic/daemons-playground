# WHAT'S NEW and HELP

## WHAT'S NEW

**Each release's notes, newest first** -- up to the last twelve -- as DAEMONS writes them in each release's
`RELEASE_NOTES.md`: what changed in the engine and the design since the release before, every change linked to its
commit. The notes' table of patches is for patching a cart, not for playing here, so the tab leaves it off.

**When a newer release lands while you play**, the page says so in a strip at the top, which you can dismiss. It never
swaps the game under you: the next game you start plays the new release.

Releases are numbered for the design they were built against -- `v11.315.3` is the third release under design bible
v11.315. How they are made, and the patches published with each:
[Releases](https://github.com/CodeMusic/DAEMONS/wiki/Releases) on the DAEMONS wiki.

## HELP

On the start page and in its own tab:

- **how to play** -- the keys, the buttons on a phone, saving and downloading;
- **the companion** -- SEND, SYNC, care, CALL HOME;
- **where else a daemon can live** -- the companion's app on an iPhone or an iPad, and the LilyGO and M5Stack boards
  ([the companion's wiki](https://github.com/CodeMusic/daemons-companion/wiki)).
